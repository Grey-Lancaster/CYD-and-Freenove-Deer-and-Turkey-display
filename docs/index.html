About

An animated autumn woods for the Freenove FNK0104S, the Freenove 2.8" and the CYD 2.8": a buck, two does, a fawn, a tom turkey and two hens wander the forest floor between golden trees while leaves drift down. Tap the screen to drop a pile of corn — the nearest animals walk over and eat it. A woods ambience (crows, turkeys and birds) plays through the speaker, with a clock and date in the corner and an alarm clock that brightens the woods like a sunrise.

Version v1.0.0. WiFi is set up on the screen the first time it starts (pick your network, type the password). After that, open http://deerhunt.local (Freenove 2.8": http://deerhunt28.local, CYD: http://cyddeerhunt.local) for settings — sound and volume, time zone and date format, 12/24-hour time, the alarm (days, snooze, loudness), flipping the screen, and swap red/blue and invert colours for CYD panels. BOOT shows the IP and status; BOOT during WiFi setup runs the woods offline. The CYD asks you to touch five crosshairs once to calibrate the touch screen.

Updating without losing WiFi: download the Deer-Hunt-<your board>-update.bin file from the latest release, open the board's web page, and use Firmware update. To set the alarm, tap the time on the screen. A minute before it rings the woods brighten and every animal stops to look up; then the chime plays over a big clock until you tap Stop or Snooze.

Source and build notes: CYD-and-Freenove-Deer-and-Turkey-display. The woods ambience is derived from a recording by Jon5515, licensed CC BY 4.0 (shortened, noise-reduced and looped).

Firmware

This replaces whatever was on the board before. Pick the one that matches your board. The two CYD builds differ only in the display driver. This installer wipes the board, including its saved WiFi. To update a board that is already set up, use the update file instead (see below) — it keeps WiFi and settings.

CYD colours wrong (negative, or red and blue swapped)? You most likely picked the wrong CYD build — try the other one (or use the swap red/blue and invert colours switches on the board's web page). Nothing is damaged.

2

Connect your board

Plug the device into a USB port, then click connect and pick it from the list. This requires the board to be in bootloader mode (it's what actually flashes it) — to just read logs from a board that's already running normally, use View Device Logs below instead, no bootloader mode needed.
